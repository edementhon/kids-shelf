# Changes

Each release's section here becomes the top of its GitHub release notes (scripts/release-finish.sh copies it there and refuses to publish a version without one). Keep each paragraph and list item on one line: GitHub release notes turn every line break into a visible one.

## v0.3.11

**Follow a YouTube playlist or channel.** Paste a playlist or channel link and Kids Shelf looks it up first: how many videos it has, how long they run and about how much space they need. Nothing downloads until you press Start. You choose what to get now (all of it, a channel's newest 20, or nothing yet), can skip videos longer than 10, 20 or 30 minutes or an hour, and can have Kids Shelf check for new videos every 6 hours. New videos go straight to the shelf, or wait for your yes with a link to watch them on YouTube first. A playlist's videos now go in one show named after the playlist.

**The videos page shows what is happening.** What is working now comes first, then anything that needs you, then what is waiting, in the order it will run. Conversions show how far they got and about how long is left, and each YouTube video's row follows it all the way to the shelf.

**Time's up no longer sends kids to you.** When a kid runs out of minutes, the shelf and the player no longer suggest asking a grown-up to watch with them, and "Free shows still work" appears only when the Free bin has shows in it. A new **Watch together** switch in the Minutes card takes the Watch together button away altogether if you would rather not offer it. It is on unless you switch it off.

**Four new division bosses.** Snippy the ÷3 crab, Gnawbert the ÷4 beaver, Prickles the ÷6 hedgehog and Finnegan the ÷7 shark, each right after the times-table boss of the same number. Kids who are already further along meet them next.

**Smaller fixes**

- The parent page shows which version is running, at the bottom of the page.
- If Windows or an antivirus program stops the YouTube downloader, the page says so and where to allow it, and a deleted downloader comes back by itself within half an hour.
- A YouTube download whose size check fails no longer stays stuck as running.
