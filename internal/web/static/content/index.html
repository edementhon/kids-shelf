📂 Add a folder or NAS share

Videos found in the folder appear on the shelf. NAS = a network drive, like a Synology or a Plex box.

Where do I find the path?
  • Windows: a folder like D:\Videos\Kids; a NAS share is at \\SERVER\Kids.
  • Mac: a folder like /Users/you/Movies/Kids; a NAS share appears under /Volumes/.
  • Linux: a folder like /home/you/Videos/Kids; a NAS share is usually mounted at /mnt/ or /media/.

Tip: connect the NAS first so this computer can see it, then paste its address here.

🔧 Fixing videos

Some videos cannot play well yet, so kids-shelf makes them a better copy in the background. This card lists the ones still waiting and the ones it could not fix.

📚 Offline libraries

Libraries work with no internet (great for trips). Think about space before you download: all of English Wikipedia needs about 120 GB. Wikipedia's top 100 articles: 318 MB. The kids' encyclopedia Vikidia is 74 MB.
